The Running Order

What Actually Happens Between 9 and 5

No theory session, no slide marathon. Demonstrations, then hands-on, then a build.

How an agentic assistant differs from a chatbot, what it can reach, and where a human has to stay in the loop. Enough to work confidently, not a computer science lecture.

Before You Enquire

Straight Answers

Who is this not for?
Three groups, honestly. It is not an awareness talk for sixty people — that is a different, shorter session and we run those separately. It is not for teams whose company will not pay for Claude seats, because the day only sticks if everyone can keep working the same way on Monday. And it is not a build-it-for-us engagement: your people build in the room, with help, which is the entire point.
Do we need Claude before the day?
Yes — one seat per participant, and a laptop each. Individual Pro accounts work; a Team plan costs slightly more but keeps the seats under company control so you can remove one when somebody leaves. We will tell you the current rates and you buy directly from Anthropic. We take nothing on it.
Our staff are not technical. Will they keep up?
That is the room this is designed for. Nobody writes code. Every exercise is ordinary work — a form, a report, a list, a client reply — and the afternoon build is done in plain English with the trainer at your elbow. Groups stay small so nobody is left behind.
Can you do a half day, or run it online?
The full day is what we recommend, because the morning teaches and the afternoon is where it becomes real. If a full day is impossible we can talk about a morning-only session, or an online awareness session for a larger group. Ask and we will tell you honestly which one fits.
What do we actually have at 5pm?
A first working version of something from their own job, and the confidence to keep building it out that week. A full tool is not an afternoon’s work and we will not pretend otherwise — but the hard part, starting, is done. Plus the pack, the prompts and a way to ask questions afterwards.
How soon can you run it, and what does it cost?
Usually two to three weeks from confirmation, once the planning meeting is done. Cost is quoted per person with a minimum of five, and you get it in writing on a one-page proposal before anything is committed — message us and we will send it.
